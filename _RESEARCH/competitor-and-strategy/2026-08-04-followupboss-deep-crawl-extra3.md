## What security means at Follow Up Boss
Security might not be as exciting as a new marketing campaign or lead conversion tactic, but make no mistake: data security is essential for a thriving real estate business – your clients trust you with their information.  
  
When you, in turn, trust us with the lifeblood of your operations—leads, contacts, and sensitive data—we don't take this responsibility lightly. Security isn't just a priority for us, it's a commitment.  
  
Follow Up Boss has invested in top-tier resources and controls to protect and serve our customers. Our investment is guided by a security framework that adheres to the best practices in the SaaS industry.
Data Privacy and Safety
Safeguard the privacy and confidentiality of leads, contacts, and sensitive data while delivering a top-tier product and service tailored to real estate professionals.
Service Continuity
Ensure availability of Follow Up Boss and all essential data to authorized users, empowering real estate agents and teams to operate seamlessly.
Data and Service Integrity
Helps ensure that user and customer data, vital to the real estate industry, isn’t corrupted or altered inappropriately, maintaining the integrity of every transaction.
Compliance and Best Practices
Implement processes and controls that align with current international regulatory standards and industry best practices, reflecting our commitment to excellence in real estate technology.
## Security practices you can trust
**Serious protection for your data**
Your data is encrypted with bank-level HTTPS security and backed up daily to multiple redundant data centers. Whether you're on your desktop or mobile, every connection to Follow Up Boss is a secure one.
**Storage you can count on**
We store your data in state-of-the-art facilities equipped with military-grade security protocols and round-the-clock monitoring. With continuous backups every second and stored securely for 35 days, we've built a vault for your valuable information.
Audited and approved security standards
Our commitment to security isn't just a promise; it's proven. We complete annual Google security audits and other independent assessments, ensuring our high standards meet and exceed industry expectations.
Billing information you can trust
Your financial details are handled with the same secure encryption leading banks use. With PCI-compliant networks, your billing information is in safe hands.

Security measures are implemented to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data. Encryption technologies protect your data both at rest and in transit. Customer data is backed up in alignment with industry standards to ensure continuity of service.
**Where is my data stored?**
![plus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/644fec6f9633da07ea1323cb/646639bcf734448b1aecc455_Plus.svg)![minus icon](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/644fec6f9633da07ea1323cb/64663a11c0464764c2855c95_minus.svg)

**Can integrations access my data?**
