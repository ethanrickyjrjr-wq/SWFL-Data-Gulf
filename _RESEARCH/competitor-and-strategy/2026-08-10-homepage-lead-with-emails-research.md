# Homepage must lead with EMAIL BUILDING, not search — decree + live comps (08/10/2026)

Operator decree (08/10/2026, verbatim core): *"No one really knows what we do unless they get to
Showcase... Search and data are the last things people are going to use this for, it kind of
became a search site because we had the data... we keep map and data somewhere to be clicked to
like an /r/ page, but we lead with what we do, build your emails without wasting time finding
data."* This EVOLVES (does not merely tweak) the 07/12 one-bar homepage: the "Type a place"
input-first grammar is itself what reads as a search engine. Leading with the product is DECIDED;
only the how is open.

## What the two named comps lead with (crawl4ai, live 08/10/2026)

Mailchimp homepage h1, verbatim: **"Email & SMS marketing _minus the learning curve_"** — then one
sub-line ("Drive revenue with intuitive marketing analytics, automations, and AI tools"), ONE CTA
(Start Free Trial), then social proof (4.5-star rating graphic) immediately under the CTA.
Grammar: [category] minus [pain]. No input field, no tool demo in the first screen.

Figma homepage h1, verbatim: **"The intelligent canvas for infinite creativity"** — paired with a
large literal PICTURE of the product (canvas screenshot), ONE CTA (Get started). Second beat:
"One workspace for your entire product development process." Grammar: abstract claim + literal
product image carrying the explanation.

Convergent pattern across both: (1) h1 names the CATEGORY so a stranger knows what is sold,
(2) the product is SHOWN (image/screenshot), (3) exactly one CTA, (4) proof adjacent to CTA.
Neither leads with an input field — an input-first hero is search-engine grammar.

## REFRAME (same day, operator): the pain is SAMENESS, not a data hunt

Verbatim: "Agents basically have data handed to them with MLS... all emails are the same with
limited same data sent to each and every agent. They all have CRMs, they all can design an email...
we are expanding your data, knowledge and look, all while saving time." Kills the "minus the data
hunt" headline. The sell = stick-out. Evidence assembled:
- BoldTrail hero (crawled live 08/10/2026): "AI That Lets Agents Do What They Do Best" — CRMs sell
  LEADS and speed-to-lead, never content differentiation. FUB deep-crawl (08/04) same shape.
- 08/03 email-anatomy research: 10 ESP/CRM platforms share ONE universal 5-part email skeleton +
  merge tags — the structural cause of every agent email looking identical.
- STEADY-PAINS Tier 1: sellers hand-ask for the exact per-ZIP read we compute (#3); the industry
  computes seller-stress and HIDES it (#1); agents' trust line = automate the middle, never the
  send (#5 — pre-send edit gate stays forever).
- What the MLS+CRM stack cannot attach to a listing email (all held by us as cited roots/probes):
  deed-recorded sale history, FEMA flood layers, permits, 9-yr tax history, neighborhood amenity
  distances + boundary polygons, delisting/price-cut/cancellation rates by ZIP, market direction
  narrative with citations. Recipes already consume amenities/flood/deed (lib/deliverable/recipes).
Hero direction v2: headline sells differentiation (e.g. "Emails your competition can't send."),
sub names the beyond-MLS data + citation moat + ~2-min build, CTA "Build one free", visual = real
captures. Wording = operator's call at render.
